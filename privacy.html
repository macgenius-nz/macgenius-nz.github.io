NAM Pedal Guide AmpSwitch Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

NAM Pedealboard is a local macOS virtual guitar pedalboard app. It does not require an account and does not collect personal data for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

AmpSwitch is a local macOS utility for comparing audio apps. AmpSwitch does not require an account and does not collect personal data for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

How AmpSwitch works

AmpSwitch runs on your Mac and controls audio app muting/unmuting locally. App settings (such as assignments and hotkeys) are stored on your device.

Permissions

  • Both apps require Microphone / Audio Input

Data collection

NAM Pedalboard and AmpSwitch do not collect, sell, or share personal data. They do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
