Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

NAM Pedalboard is a local macOS virtual guitar pedalboard app. It does not require an account. This website uses Plausible Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and page views.

AmpSwitch is a local macOS utility for comparing audio apps. AmpSwitch does not require an account. This website uses Plausible Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and page views.

How AmpSwitch works

AmpSwitch runs on your Mac and controls audio app muting/unmuting locally. App settings (such as assignments and hotkeys) are stored on your device.

Permissions

  • Both apps require Microphone / Audio Input

Data collection

Plausible Analytics is privacy-friendly and does not use cookies on this site. We do not use advertising trackers.
