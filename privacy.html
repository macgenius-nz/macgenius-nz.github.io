Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

AmpSwitch is a local macOS utility for comparing audio apps. AmpSwitch does not require an account and does not collect personal data for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

How AmpSwitch works

AmpSwitch runs on your Mac and controls audio app muting/unmuting locally. App settings (such as assignments and hotkeys) are stored on your device.

Permissions

  • Microphone / Audio Input: used to enable audio-control features required for AmpSwitch to manage per-app audio muting/unmuting.
  • Accessibility (optional): used only to enable global hotkeys when you choose to turn them on.

Data collection

AmpSwitch does not collect, sell, or share personal data. AmpSwitch does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
