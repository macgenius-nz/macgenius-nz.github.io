NAM Pedal Guide

User guide for the NAM Pedalboard standalone app.

Quick Start

On first launch, NAM Pedalboard loads the bundled Foundation Clean.nam model and selects the Foundation Clean scene.

Basic setup:

  • Set your audio device in the settings cog at the top right.
  • Plug in your guitar and set GLOBAL IN so the app stays clean and controlled.
  • Pick a scene and play.
  • Play! That's the whole point of this...

Advanced (well, there goes practice...) features:

  • New to NAM? let us connect you to Tone3000 (the NAM Mothership). The Pick a "Full Rig" version of your favorite amp and let the fun begin!
  • Have NAM's already? - Load a NAM with Load NAM
  • Finally, if you must ;-) load an IR. I'll show you how deep the rabbit-hole goes...

Useful defaults:

  • Foundation Clean is the main startup scene.
  • Clear All resets the app back to Foundation Clean and clears the current IR.

Scenes

Scenes are complete snapshots of the pedalboard.

Factory scenes:

  • Foundation Clean
  • Secret Slap Clean
  • BB Edge Rhythm
  • Funk Swirl
  • Continuum
  • Lead Push

User scenes:

  • You have user scene slots for your own sounds.
  • Select a user slot, then press Save to store the current board.
  • Rename changes the selected user scene name.
  • Delete clears the selected user scene slot.

What a scene stores:

  • Pedal on/off states
  • Pedal knob values
  • EQ settings
  • Trem/reverb/delay settings
  • Drive order
  • Voicing settings
  • Persistent scene output level

What a scene does not store:

  • Temporary Match offsets unless you explicitly save them
  • Global device setup
  • GLOBAL IN / GLOBAL OUT

Models and IRs

Load NAM loads a .nam model.

Use an IR when:

  • the NAM is an amp-only capture
  • you want to pair the amp with a different speaker/cabinet sound

You may not need an IR when:

  • the NAM is a full-rig capture
  • the cab/speaker sound is already baked into the model

If the NAM already sounds complete on its own, it is probably full-rig. If it sounds direct or unfinished, it probably wants an IR.

Header and Global Controls

Top bar controls: Load NAM, Tone3000, Load IR, Clear All, TUNER, STEREO, PHONES, settings cog.

Global controls:

  • GLOBAL IN — sets how hard your guitar/interface hits the pedalboard. Treat it as your instrument calibration control, not a scene level.
  • GLOBAL OUT — sets final output level. Treat it as your monitor/recording level.

Calibration (Cal)

The header shows the current model's auto-level offset, e.g. Cal +3.2 dB. NAM models vary widely in output level — Cal measures the model and applies a gain offset automatically so switching models doesn't surprise you volume-wise. Toggle it in the settings cog menu.

PHONES

Activates headphone-optimised processing. Use it when listening on headphones rather than monitors or an amp.

STEREO and Ø B

STEREO activates biamp mode, routing the signal to a stereo pair with separate processing. Ø B flips the phase of the right channel — useful for aligning phase in biamp mode or checking mono compatibility.

Pedals

Comp

A musical guitar compressor. Controls: Sustain, Blend, Level. Best for clean scenes, subtle bloom before the Boost, or evening out attack without getting squashy.

Boost

Clean boost or front-end push. Controls: Boost, Vol. Use it to wake up a clean sound, add edge before the NAM, or push BB or Gold Drive.

BB

The warmer, softer low-gain drive. Controls: Gain, Tone, Vol. Character: rounder, thicker, more amp-like.

Gold Drive

The tighter, more articulate low-gain drive. Controls: Gain, Treble, Out. Character: clearer, tighter, more forward.

Drive Order

The DRIVE tile switches the order of BB and Gold Drive. Use BB after Gold Drive for softer edge, or Gold Drive after BB for more focused cut.

Chorus

Warm stereo chorus. Controls: Rate, Depth. Subtle stereo spread at low depth, more pronounced shimmer higher up. Good for adding movement to clean tones or layering under reverb.

Delay

Analogue-voiced echo for slap, space, and halo. Controls: Delay, Fdbk, Mix.

Trem / Reverb

TremVerb combines tremolo and reverb in one section.

Tremolo: Rate, Depth, and three mode buttons — Tube, Photocell, Harmonic.

Reverb: Decay, Mix, Tone, and three algorithms — Plate, Hall, Spring.

Voicing

Shapes the finished tone after the cabinet stage.

  • Sweeten — harmonic gloss and tonal bloom
  • Deep — thickens the low-end foundation
  • Bright — opens up the top-end air

Cab Voice

Adds cabinet character on top of your IR — or instead of one.

  • Flat — no colouration
  • 1x12 — open-back, looser and more airy
  • 2x12 — semi-open, balanced and versatile
  • 4x12 — closed-back, tighter and more focused

Pair with Deep and Bright to fine-tune low-end weight and top-end openness.

EQ

Your main voicing section, not a generic utility EQ. Bands: 150 Hz, 425 Hz, 1.8k, 4.8k, Master.

Use it to add body, reduce poke, add shimmer, or fine-tune scene balance. Post-IR is the main finishing position for most players.

Match / Loudness

Use Match to level-balance scenes so switching doesn't catch you out volume-wise.

  1. Select the reference scene and press Set Ref.
  2. Play — the app captures the next active phrase.
  3. Switch to the target scene and press Apply.
  4. Play again — the app calculates a temporary offset.
  5. Press Save to commit the offset into the scene permanently.

Match is temporary until you press Save. Reset clears the temporary state.

Tone3000

Browse and load NAM models from inside the app — search by amp type, browse featured tones, connect to your Tone3000 account. If you just want local files, ignore it and use Load NAM.

Troubleshooting

Sounds too quiet

  • The NAM itself may be quiet
  • Check GLOBAL OUT
  • Check the selected scene output level
  • You may have loaded an amp-only NAM without an IR

Sounds harsh

  • Lower GLOBAL IN
  • Reduce drive pedal output
  • Lower Sweeten
  • Reduce 4.8k
  • Use less Bright

One scene is louder than another

Use the Match workflow — Set Ref, Apply, then Save.

Clean tones feel too stiff

  • Turn Comp on
  • A little Boost
  • A little Sweeten
  • A touch more 425 Hz
